# Speech fixtures — licenses & attribution

These files are **not** Chirp-original. They are redistributed for offline ASR
regression tests under their original licenses.

## normal_ldc93s1.wav

- Source: NIST / TIMIT-derived “LDC93S1” smoke utterance, widely distributed
  (e.g. Mozilla DeepSpeech `data/smoke_test/LDC93S1.wav`).
- Transcript: “She had your dark suit in greasy wash water all year.”
- Converted to 16 kHz mono IEEE float32 WAV for Chirp’s pipeline.

## librispeech_*.wav

- Source: [LibriSpeech](https://www.openslr.org/12) (and mini_librispeech
  [OpenSLR-31](https://www.openslr.org/31/)) read speech from LibriVox audiobooks.
- License: **CC BY 4.0** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Citation: Panayotov et al., “Librispeech: An ASR corpus based on public domain
  audio books,” ICASSP 2015.
- Files extracted from `dev-clean-2`, converted to 16 kHz mono IEEE float32 WAV.
- Official transcripts are uppercase; tests normalize before WER scoring.

Do not replace with proprietary recordings. Prefer OpenSLR / LibriSpeech /
CC0 Common Voice when adding more.
